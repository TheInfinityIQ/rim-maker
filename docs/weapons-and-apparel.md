# Weapon types, melee and grenades, and apparel implementation

This review uses the installed 1.6.4871 XML and assembly. `scripts/extract-equipment.py` resolves official XML inheritance into `equipmentCatalog.json` (106 weapon/improvised/turret definitions and 112 apparel definitions) and `weaponTemplates.json`. The **Equipment guide** in RimMaker exposes the inventory and source XML paths. This is an unpatched snapshot of explicit XML definitions across Core and five installed expansions; dynamically generated definitions, Workshop mods and arbitrary abilities are not an exhaustive part of those counts.

## Implemented editor types

**Ranged:** twenty game-derived starting templates cover common pistols, shotguns, rifles, SMGs, machine guns, charge weapons, bows/pila and incendiary/smoke/EMP launchers. They retain their own verbs, projectile properties, graphics, sounds, stats and recipes. All families also have an explicit custom/reference-baseline choice. See [editor consistency](editor-consistency.md) for current form organization.

**Melee:** club, knife, gladius, mace, longsword, ikwa and spear. Templates retain their actual parent, stats, crafting research/bench/skill requirements, material categories, ingredient counts, graphic paths and tools. Combat edits expose all tool capacities (including multiple capacities per attack), power, cooldown, optional armour penetration and relative selection weight. No ranged verb or projectile is exported. Quality support follows the chosen parent: the stock club lacks the quality comp, while quality templates retain it. Variable stuff crafting uses `costStuffCount` and `stuffCategories`; fixed `costList` entries are additional ingredients. Turning off variable material requires fixed ingredients. The game derives material/quality-adjusted melee damage and cooldown through `Tool` and stat workers; no browser melee hit or DPS simulator is claimed.

**Grenades:** frag, molotov, EMP, and Biotech tox. Each uses `BaseMakeableGrenade`, `Verb_LaunchProjectile`, range 12.9, forced scatter 1.9, warmup 1.5, thrown combat rulepack, target-location permission, stock throw/handling sounds and the source recipe. Projectile profiles preserve slow travel and arc, frag/EMP fuses, explosive radius, fire/fuel or gas properties. Stock flare/secondary item explosion comps are preserved through XML Class attributes where present. Vanilla equipped grenades remain reusable; one-use behavior is a separate system. These profiles can be edited or switched to an existing projectile. Native accuracy/effect measurement stays available; an ordinary bullet hit curve is not assigned to thrown/scattered grenades.

All types coexist with existing ranged weapons in the same ZIP. The existing `Weapons_Ranged.xml` filename is retained for compatibility with prior consumers; it can contain all three equipment types. Export clones preserve additional verb fields and XML attributes without serializing editor metadata or modifying the user's editable model. Template expansion requirements are included even when the projectile is authored as a custom copy.

## Other weapon families

- Projectile guns and bows include low-tech bows/pila, pistols, SMGs, shotguns, rifles, machine guns, charge weapons and projectile-based energy weapons. Explosive, incendiary, EMP, smoke, firefoam, tox and specialised ammunition do not all follow ordinary bullet rules. Many fit the ranged editor using the correct existing projectile/profile.
- `Verb_ShootOneUse` drives triple/doomsday rocket launchers and consumes the equipment; it needs explicit one-use controls.
- `Verb_ShootBeam` (graser), `Verb_SpewFire` (mini flameblaster), `Verb_ArcSprayIncinerator` and `Verb_ArcSprayProjectile` (firefoam turret) have dedicated targeting, path, spray or continuous effects. They are inventoried, not enabled by changing a bullet class.
- Special melee includes breach weapons, monoswords, plasmaswords, zeushammers, psyfocus staffs, persona/bladelink weapons and expansion-specific modifiers. Extra damage, comps, bonding, traits, abilities and targeting require controls beyond ordinary tool power.
- Improvised item weapons include beer, wood, tusks and horns. The inventory uses the game's `ThingDef.IsWeapon` distinction: non-apparel Items with verbs or tools. Not every such item has a Primary equipment role or is an appropriate human-craftable template.
- Turrets and artillery need a Building definition linked to a gun, plus applicable power/fuel/ammunition, manning, reload and targeting behavior. Mortar guns and mech-only weapons are not ordinary handheld templates.
- Worn launchers and utility packs belong to apparel/comps/abilities. Implants use HediffDefs, natural attacks use race/body tools, and psycasts or other offensive abilities use their own definitions. Unique/legendary weapons add generation and traits. These systems need dedicated editors and runtime tests; the current catalog does not claim to enumerate every offensive AbilityDef or HediffDef.

## How armour and wearables are authored

Start from a real `ThingDef` parent such as `ApparelMakeableBase` (tailoring), `ArmorSmithableBase` (smithy), `ArmorMachineableBase` (machining), or the appropriate headgear/utility parent. Apparel is an Item with an Apparel-derived class, apparel properties, comps and stats. RecipeMaker configures its workbench, work, research and skills. Fixed and stuff-based ingredients follow the same distinction as melee crafting.

`apparel.bodyPartGroups` defines actual coverage, while `apparel.layers` defines worn layering and compatibility. Core layers are OnSkin, Middle, Shell, Belt, Overhead and EyeCover. Check `ApparelUtility` for compatible pairs, exclusive rules and overlapping groups rather than equating the sprite with coverage. Body type, developmental stage, gender, head/hair concealment and expansion requirements may further restrict use.

Armour uses `ArmorRating_Sharp`, `ArmorRating_Blunt`, `ArmorRating_Heat`, with 1 meaning 100%; apparel can also specify insulation, equip delay, mass, hit points, and equipped stat offsets. `Verse.ArmorUtility.GetPostArmorDamage` iterates covering worn apparel before natural armour. At each layer it subtracts penetration, then rolls against half/full effective armour: deflection, half damage, or full passage. Reduced sharp damage can become blunt for subsequent layers. The armour item also takes durability damage. This is not a flat percentage damage reduction; body part, damage category, material, quality, durability and special workers matter.

Ground/inventory `graphicData.texPath` and `apparel.wornGraphicPath` are separate roles. Worn assets must satisfy the chosen renderer's facing/body-type naming, and may need child/body-specific variants, masks and stuff-aware shaders. Headgear and utility gear can have different render nodes. Shield belts, jump/firefoam/smokepop packs and integrated weapons additionally need their actual subclasses, comps, abilities, charges and restrictions. A future apparel editor should test these rendering and equipment behaviors as well as coverage and damage.

Useful source examples: Core `ThingDefs_Misc/Apparel_Various.xml` (T-shirts, flak vest, power armour and parents), `Apparel_Headgear.xml`, `Apparel_Belts.xml`, `Apparel_Packs.xml`, `Bodies/BodyPartGroups.xml`, `Misc/ApparelLayerDefs/ApparelLayerDefs.xml`, and `Stats/Stats_Apparel.xml`. The searchable guide supplies each individual definition's exact pack/path. Apparel research is implemented in the guide; apparel creation/export is intentionally not presented as finished.

## Verification

`npm run test:unit -- --run` checks all 31 templates, mixed archives, material fields, dependencies, valid XML, extra verb fields and clone safety. Browser tests create/edit/export a melee weapon and a fused grenade together and exercise the guide. `npm run test:ingame:weapon-types` exports all 31 templates through the production archive builder, loads them in an isolated Core+Biotech profile and tests recipe product generation, material, equipping, graphics, each melee verb, native throwing/projectile/fuse lifecycle, reusable equipment, controlled frag damage, actual mechanoid EMP stun, molotov fire, tox gas and dropping. It checks startup errors and cleans its exact temporary mod path. It does not certify balance, arbitrary custom properties, all materials, perceptual audio, other mods, or every combat situation.

## NPC equipment tags

The Weapon tab includes an NPC equipment tags panel for ranged, melee and grenade definitions. Choose official tags or enter exact custom tags to make the weapon a candidate for pawn kinds that use them. Template tags can be restored or cleared. An empty list exports with inheritance disabled, so it excludes the item from ordinary tagged NPC equipment selection.

The displayed faction types are tag matches. Equipment budgets, allowed materials, capabilities and other native generation rules still apply. Tags are not a faction ownership restriction and do not change trader stock or existing pawns. These inputs export to `ThingDef/weaponTags/li`.
